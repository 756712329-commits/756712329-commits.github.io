About This Platform

We operate an automation-driven publishing hub for Chinese-speaking readers worldwide. All content is produced by our in-house data pipeline (codename AutoFarm) from public, official APIs — refreshed continuously without manual curation:

  • Funding-Rate Dashboard (/) — aggregates USDT perpetual funding rates across Binance, OKX, Bybit and Gate every 10 minutes, ranked by annualized yield.
  • PC Game Deals Radar (/deals.html) — daily price aggregation across 30+ official PC storefronts, quality-ranked discounts and all-time-low alerts.
  • Open-Source AI Tools Index (/ai/) — weekly ranking built on real GitHub star-growth and HuggingFace download data.
  • Daily Tech Briefing (/brief/latest.html) — a curated Chinese-language digest of Hacker News, release notes and GitHub Trending, delivered by opt-in e-mail.

How We Work With Merchants

Where we link to exchanges or stores, we may use affiliate tracking links provided by the merchant or its network. Such links are contextually embedded inside relevant content and are marked rel="sponsored". Editorial selection is fully automated from market data and is never influenced by commercial relationships — merchants cannot pay for placement.

Promotional methods we do NOT use: paid search bidding on merchant trademarks, cookie stuffing, incentified/cashback redirection, coupon-code scraping of other publishers, or unsolicited mass messaging. Traffic comes from organic search, RSS/e-mail subscribers and community sharing.
